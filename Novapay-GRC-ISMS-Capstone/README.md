# GRC Audit and ISMS Capstone: NovaPay Financial Services

A GRC audit and ISO/IEC 27001:2022 ISMS readiness project, completed as the capstone for my cybersecurity programme. The case is a fictional fintech, NovaPay, with a 3 million-user payments app, 500+ merchant API integrations and operations across West and Southern Africa. Its governance did not keep pace with its growth.

## What this project covers

A desk-based audit that finds the governance gaps, rates the risks, and designs the ISMS documents and a 90-day roadmap to close them. It is a readiness assessment, not a certification audit.

**Key results**
- 10 risks assessed on a 5 × 5 matrix: 5 Critical before treatment, none Critical or High after the roadmap
- 8 priority findings, each with an owner and due date
- 20 ISO 27001 Annex A controls selected and mapped to NIST CSF 2.0 and PCI DSS v4.0.1
- A 90-day plan and 12-month roadmap with KPIs

## The six deliverables

| # | Deliverable | Type | Document no. |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Scope Statement | Updated | NP-ISMS-DOC-001 |
| 2 | Leadership Commitment Statement | Updated | NP-ISMS-DOC-002 |
| 3 | Risk Assessment and Treatment Procedure | Updated | NP-PRO-RAT-001 |
| 4 | Information Security Policy | New | NP-POL-ISP-001 |
| 5 | Incident Response Policy | New | NP-POL-IRP-001 |
| 6 | Statement of Applicability (20 controls) | Updated | NP-ISMS-DOC-003 |

The deck also includes an executive summary, audit terms of reference, priority findings, a framework crosswalk, a risk register and treatment plan, an audit conclusion by ISO 27001 clause, and references.
